# ADR-029 — Historical PIT preparation, visibility and UNKNOWN

Status: accepted engineering boundary, business parameters UNSET_REQUIRED.
Version1.0.0; supplements immutable ADR004/009 without replacing them.

Selectors expose OBSERVED_AT_TIME (available_at and retrieved_at <= cutoff)
and HISTORICAL_AVAILABILITY_RECONSTRUCTION (independent historical availability
proof, not observed then). DATE_ONLY/UNKNOWN publication has no invented instant.
event_time may describe a future effective corporate action, so no universal
event <= published <= available total order is asserted. Publication cannot
postdate availability where the particular product requires public disclosure.

Late financial revisions and their metadata/counts/hashes do not change a
previous frozen visible prefix. Conflicting visible revisions remain UNKNOWN;
update_flag/current query is not first-publication chronology. Status requires
ST, suspension, limits, delist, listed and universe effective intervals plus
visible complete evidence. Current membership and absent rows prove nothing
historical. Actual source is quarantined; no actual historical proof issuer
exists. Positive selector results are synthetic fixtures, never live authority.
